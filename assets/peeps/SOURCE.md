# Open Peeps assets

49 individual pose illustrations downloaded directly from the official site's
own CDN-hosted SVG downloads (not regenerated, not a design-tool export):
https://www.openpeeps.com/

- **Creator:** Pablo Stanley
- **License:** CC0 1.0 — free for commercial and personal use, no attribution
  required. (Credited here anyway as good practice.)
- Files are untouched originals: black fill/outline + white highlight only
  (`fill="#000000"`, `fill="#FFFFFF"`, `fill="none"`), no color added or
  removed.
- Filenames (`peep-N.svg`) match the names openpeeps.com itself uses.

Currently used on the site: `peep-7.svg` (cover screen hero illustration).
The rest are downloaded for reuse in later sections rather than re-fetching
one at a time.
